## iDEA

- Brand tailored to who we are
  - Use the Estonia brand, values (smart, digital, flexible, fast, solution-oriented)
  - Be faster logistically (faster communication, faster delivery etc. etc.)
  - Be cheaper (not meaning cheap as worse quality, but more efficient - we have less overhead, no offices, work costs less in Est compared to in Ger)
  - Be smart
- Be personal:
  - All competitor website are very impersonal (stock copy, images) - add video to the website of Steven 
  - Be reliable through who we are
  - Make it reliable not through numbers/clients (we dont have them), but who we are: video presenting company, way we work
- Website:
- Current Black - gold is a bit heavy :/ Example of a lighter site: https://brand.estonia.ee/
